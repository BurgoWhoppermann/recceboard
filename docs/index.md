# Recce Board

**A recce board for commercial directors.** Record shots on location, trim them
on the spot, arrange them on a free canvas, and play the whole board back as a
film. iPhone, iPad and Mac — one app, one purchase.

## What it does

**Record and cut in one go.** The camera opens with the board's format already
framed. Stop recording and you're in the trim, not on a menu. Keep it, or shoot
the next one — a series of shots is a series of taps.

**A board, not a list.** Shots sit where you put them on an open canvas. Reading
order comes from the arrangement, the way a page reads: left to right, top to
bottom. Put two shots side by side and they become a scene.

**Play it as a film.** The board runs as a cut, with the shots at their real
lengths and stills held for as long as you set. Record a voice-over over the
whole thing, or over a single shot. Lay music underneath.

**Send it on.** Export the film as an MP4, the board as a contact-sheet PDF, or
every shot as its own file. Frame lines — 2.39, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16 — are burned
into whatever leaves the app, so what you send matches what you framed.

**On all your devices.** Boards mirror through your own iCloud account. Start on
the phone at the location, finish on the Mac at the desk.

**Together, if you want.** Share a board with a six-character code. Everyone with
the code can add shots and rearrange.

## Requirements

iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26. An iCloud account for syncing between your own
devices (the app works without one, on that device only).
