# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 August 2026_

Recce Board is made by Carlo Oppermann. This policy says what the app does with
your information. It is short because the app does very little.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The developer of Recce Board receives no data from the app. There is
no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no
third-party SDK of any kind in the app.

## Where your boards live

Your boards — the shots, photos, notes, drawings, voice-overs and music you add —
are stored **on your device**, and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in **your own
private iCloud database**. That is your Apple account, not ours. We cannot see
it, and neither can Apple, beyond what is described in Apple's own iCloud
privacy terms.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device and nothing
leaves it.

## Sharing a board

If you choose to share a board, a six-character code is stored in a public Apple
CloudKit database. That record contains the board's title and a link that lets
someone join the share. It expires after a week. Nothing else about the board is
public, and the board's contents remain in Apple's shared-database mechanism
between you and the people you invited.

Don't put anything in a board title that you wouldn't want a stranger to read,
since a title travels with the invite.

## Camera, microphone and photo library

The app asks for the camera to record shots, the microphone to record their
sound and your voice-overs, and the photo library when you import from it. What
these produce goes into your board, on your device and in your own iCloud. It is
never sent anywhere else.

If you switch on saving captures to Photos, recordings are also written to your
own photo library.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous
version stays in the project's public history.
